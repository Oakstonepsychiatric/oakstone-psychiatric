Our Philosophy & Care Principles

Rooted in Compassion.
Grounded in Expertise.

Founded by a board-certified psychiatrist who has cared for patients and families throughout Visalia and California's Central Valley for more than a decade, Oakstone Psychiatric Practice combines academic expertise with personalized, compassionate care.

At Oakstone Psychiatric Practice, we believe exceptional psychiatric care begins with clinical excellence, thoughtful relationships, and an unwavering commitment to doing what is right for every patient.

Our practice provides comprehensive psychiatric care for patients of all ages, including adults, adolescents, and children throughout Visalia and the Central Valley. We combine the latest advances in psychiatric medicine with individualized treatment, recognizing that every person's story, goals, and needs are unique at every stage of life.

Our physicians and clinicians provide comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and personalized treatment for a broad range of mental health conditions, including depression, anxiety, ADHD, bipolar disorder, trauma-related disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and treatment-resistant conditions.

At Oakstone, we take the time to understand each patient as a whole person, building trusted relationships that support meaningful, lasting improvement. Every recommendation is guided by scientific evidence, clinical judgment, and genuine compassion.

Expert Clinical Leadership

Our Co-founders

Our board-certified specialists and experienced clinical staff work collaboratively to provide empathetic, high-quality care across all ages.

Medical Director & Co-founder

Cory Daniel Jaques, M.D.

Board-Certified Adult, Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist

Dr. Cory Daniel Jaques is the co-founder and Medical Director of Oakstone Psychiatric Practice, where he leads a team of dedicated psychiatric clinicians committed to delivering exceptional, evidence-based mental health care throughout Visalia and California's Central Valley.

After earning his medical degree from the University of Minnesota Medical School, Dr. Jaques completed both his General Psychiatry Residency and Child & Adolescent Psychiatry Fellowship at the UCLA Semel Institute for Neuroscience and Human Behavior, one of the nation's leading centers for psychiatric education and research.

For more than a decade, Dr. Jaques has provided psychiatric care to children, adolescents, and adults across the Central Valley. In addition to leading Oakstone Psychiatric Practice, he oversees our certified REMS Spravato® clinic, providing relief for patients experiencing treatment-resistant depression.

Co-founder

April Ortiz, PMHNP-BC

Board-Certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

April Ortiz is a co-founder and board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner at Oakstone Psychiatric Practice with many years of experience providing compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care across Visalia and the Central Valley.

At Oakstone, April provides comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and individualized treatment across the lifespan for adults, adolescents, and children experiencing a wide range of mental health conditions.

Working closely with Dr. Cory Daniel Jaques, April practices within the clinical philosophy that defines Oakstone: delivering personalized, ethical, and evidence-based psychiatric care with professionalism, compassion, and respect.
