Understanding Treatment-Resistant Depression

When Depression Hasn’t Responded,
There May Be Another Path.

Millions of individuals endure major depressive disorder without experiencing sustained remission from standard antidepressants. If you have tried two or more antidepressants without adequate relief, you may be experiencing what psychiatrists classify as Treatment-Resistant Depression (TRD).

Beyond the Serotonin Waiting Game

Standard oral antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs) focus on monoamines like serotonin and norepinephrine, frequently requiring 6 to 8 weeks to determine efficacy. When these pathways fail to respond, a biologically different target is needed.

FDA-Approved Esketamine Nasal Spray

SPRAVATO® (esketamine) is the S-enantiomer of ketamine, formulated as a controlled nasal spray. It is specifically approved by the US FDA for adults with Treatment-Resistant Depression and MDD with acute suicidal ideation.

Safety Under Expert Clinical Care

Because Spravato carries unique temporary physiological effects, it is never taken at home. Every single treatment is monitored by psychiatric medical personnel in our certified Visalia center to ensure complete safety and reassurance.

INDICATION: SPRAVATO® (esketamine) CIII nasal spray is indicated, in conjunction with an oral antidepressant, for the treatment of treatment-resistant depression (TRD) in adults and depressive symptoms in adults with major depressive disorder (MDD) with acute suicidal ideation or behavior.

IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION: SPRAVATO® can cause serious side effects including sedation, dissociation, and increased blood pressure. Because of these risks, SPRAVATO® is only available through a restricted program called the SPRAVATO® REMS and must be administered under direct medical supervision at certified healthcare facilities such as Oakstone Psychiatric Practice. Patients must be observed for at least 2 hours following each administration.
